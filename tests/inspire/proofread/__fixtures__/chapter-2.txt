The thing about setbacks is that they accumulate... not in dramatic ways, but in the small adjustments Maria made each morning at her desk.

Carol Dweck wrote in her 2006 book that "The view you adopt for yourself profoundly affects the way you lead your life" — and Maria had been adopting the wrong view for months.

She started keeping a notebook. Not the project management kind — this was different. Each morning she'd write three things she noticed. The way light fell on her Boston office window. The particular shade of green her pothos showed in February.

The pothos was still there on her desk, growing despite everything. Despite the cold snap in January. Despite the fact that Maria forgot to water it for two weeks during the Henderson project.

What Carol Dweck called the growth mindset — Maria just called it showing up.
