## Alignment Warnings Analysis

The sentence segmenter produced **96 positional alignment warnings** plus 4 out-of-bounds warnings for sentence 16. These occur because:

1. **Hyphenated words:** The STT transcribes `mic-drop` as two tokens `mic`/`drop`, causing downstream token shifts
2. **Filler tokens:** STT picks up `"n"` and `"1"` as filler words (Chirp artifact) that don't appear in the original text
3. **Compound words:** `"afternoons"` → `"afternoon"` (singular/plural mismatch from STT)
4. **Sentence 16 overflow:** The last sentence (`"That's the whole secret"`) refers to word indices 109–112, but only 109 word timings exist. This causes sentence 16 to span the full composition (0–51.8s frames)

**Impact:** The alignment warnings are cosmetic — the segmenter uses positional (not semantic) alignment, so timing is correct per-position even when tokens don't match exactly. Sentence 16's full-duration span is clamped by the Remotion composition boundary.

1. **The narration is stylistically strong:** Chirp 3 HD prosody marks (`...`, `—`, `( )`) create natural pauses and emotional emphasis
2. **Sentence 16 is a known edge case:** The STT token count exceeds the word timing array, causing it to span the full composition — this is a pipeline bug that should be addressed
3. **Clip 0 timing is tight at 1.7s:** The `crowd standing ovation` clip starts at frame 6 and must cover just 51 frames — the fade transition may overlap significantly
4. **Video quality varies:** Clip 2 (steady river) is 98 MB vs Clip 0 at 9.2 MB — encoding bitrates differ substantially across Pixabay sources
5. **Loop flag on clip 4:** The `morning walk journey` video is shorter than the needed duration and will loop, which may look unnatural depending on scene content
