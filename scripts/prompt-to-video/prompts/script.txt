## Narrative Structure (all 10 beats required)
1. **Cold hook** ({hookType} type) — Open with a contradiction, cost-of-ignorance, or hidden-mechanism statement that grabs attention immediately
2. **Viewer promise** — Tell the viewer what they'll know by the end ("By the end you'll know...")
3. **Context** — Why this matters now, and why it matters to this viewer specifically
4. **Baseline explanation** — The simple version of what's happening
5. **Mechanism** — What's actually happening underneath, the real drivers
6. **Evidence** — Use a claim → evidence → implication beat. Every 30-60 seconds, introduce a new piece of evidence
7. **Tradeoffs** — When this works, when it fails, who it applies to, who it doesn't
8. **Practical framework** — A rule of thumb, decision tree, or checklist the viewer can apply
9. **Final judgment** — An opinionated, clear conclusion. Take a stance
10. **Closing loop** — Resolve the opening hook. Come full circle

## Format Selection
Self-select the best format for this topic and STAMP it on your output:

- **Format A (Myth vs reality)**: People believe X → X is partly true → X misses Y → evidence says Z → use this rule instead
- **Format B (Investigation)**: Claim sounds simple → depends on hidden variables → test each → some fail → one or two survive → final recommendation
- **Format C (Explainer)**: Visible behavior → hidden mechanism → why misunderstood → correct mental model → how to apply
- **Format D (Decision framework)**: Option A for X → Option B for Y → avoid C unless Z → decision tree

## Narration Rules
- Be precise but conversational. Natural speech patterns, not formal writing
- No "Today we're talking about..." or "In this video..." openings
- No definitions-first — lead with the interesting part, define terms only when needed
- Use short sentences. One idea per sentence
- Each narrative beat is roughly {secondsPerBeat} seconds of spoken narration (about {wordsPerBeat} words)
